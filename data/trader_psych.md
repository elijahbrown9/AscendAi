### Where the leaks are (ranked by dollars this week)
1. **One-ticker fixation.** RDDT: 11 round trips in 4 sessions, −$967.49,
   CHURN ×9 + REVENGE ×2, ending in a self-imposed indefinite ban. The

   watch for the fixation MIGRATING to a new host ticker (SNDK is the

2. **Conviction inflates size past every cap.** RDDT 7/27 (~4u vs 3u cap),
   SNDK 7/30 (~full equity vs 30% cap). Both times the trade WON, which
   is the dangerous part: the lesson reinforced was "caps are for other
   days." No written plan existed for either size.
3. **Session-boundary trades lose.** Every overnight-session violation this
   week lost money (MU round trip −$151.71, SQQQ ×2 −$25.30). Premarket
   entries were also net negative except SNDK. The 8pm-4am ban exists for
   this trader specifically, not as a generality.
4. **Stated stance and same-day action diverge under a moving tape.**
   Wednesday: declared "cash unless puts/shorts, much lower to go" — then
   bought RDDT, BE, and QQQI long the same afternoon (−$883 combined).
   The declaration was the top-tick of the bearish feeling, not a plan.
   Treat strongly-worded stance declarations as sentiment data, not as
   standing orders — and surface the divergence the moment it appears.
5. **Losses trigger self-abuse language, then MORE trading.** "I'm
   retarded" (7/27, after overnight RDDT exits) preceded four more RDDT
   round trips. "I've done it again" (7/29) preceded the biggest loss
   day. The tell is the language: when a message contains self-directed
   contempt, the next trades are the highest-risk of the week. The
   agent's job at that moment: slow the tempo — surface the daily-loss
   framework and the P2 questions, never pile on, never cheerlead.
